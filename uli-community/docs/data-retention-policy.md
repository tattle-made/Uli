# Data Retention Policy

This policy outlines the internal management process for retention of data shared by users on the Uli app. 

**General Principles for Processing Data**
- Only data that is necessary for the function and performance of the app must be collected, stored and processed by the app.
- To the extent permissible, data collection, especially personal data collection must be minimised. 
- Data must not be stored for an indefinite period; please identify and establish minimum storage requirements for data collected from the user.
- Implement reasonable technical safeguards for retention of data, in accordance with industry standards and practices.

**Retention timelines of data in India**
- Access logs, traffic data, system records: Minimum period of 1 (one) year.
- Inactive user accounts: May be retained for a maximum period of 3 (three) years, following which they must mandatorily be deleted.
- Social media account access information: Given the sensitive nature of information and access provided, it is recommended that the personal data not be retained beyond the period of 1 (one) year. As a precautionary measure, a notice may be sent to the user profile every quarter to confirm they consent to continued access to the personal data.
