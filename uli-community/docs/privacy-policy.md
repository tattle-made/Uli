# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy (“Policy”), governs how we will handle the data you share with us through the app.

We (Tattle Civic Technologies Pvt Ltd) are a civic tech organisation registered in and operating in accordance with the laws of India. This app has been developed to assist adults in detecting abuse on social media feeds, and help them access support networks.

Data We Collect:
- Social media account information  
- Name  
- Email ID  

We collect this information from you for the purpose of providing you with our services. You can revoke your consent to sharing this data with us at any point.
The data you share with us will be used to:
Access your public social media profile (but not your DMs unless you explicitly provide us with permission to do so)
Scan your social media feed, and flag instances where abuse has been detected; this will include comments on your profile, and public posts and comments on your feed

The app only detects abusive content and notifies you of the threshold, it does not access or store personal data of other users other than their username on the platform. Any action, whether pursued or not, will be taken by You. 

Sharing and Disclosure
We do not disclose or share any of your personal information with any third party vendors, or analytics tools. 

Erasure
We will delete your personal data once we receive a deletion request from you. You may submit a request for deletion on the app, or email us at support@tattle.co.in. Pursuant to receipt of email, you will receive an acknowledgement, following which we will commence the process of deletion. Your data will be deleted within 7 business days, and you will receive a notification 48 hours before it is being deleted. A confirmation email will be sent to you once the deletion process has been completed.

Retention
We may be required to retain some of your data to comply with the retention requirements under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, CERT-In directions and other applicable law and regulation governing data retention in India. We will retain your system and processing logs for a minimum period of 1 (one) year for security audits and breach investigation purposes. 

Security
In accordance with applicable law and regulations including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we will undertake reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect the personal data that you share with us on this app. In spite of this, there may be instances where your data is subject to unauthorised access or disclosure; in such an event, we will notify the relevant authorities in accordance with the timelines prescribed under applicable laws and regulations in India and undertake measures to respond to these instances.

You will be notified of the breach through the registered email ID you share with us,with information about the nature of the breach and any consequences it may have upon you. We will communicate the measures we will be taking to mitigate risks. We will also provide you with details of safety measures you may be required to take following such incidents.
