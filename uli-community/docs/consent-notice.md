# Consent Notice

We are committed to keeping our Uli community safe. For detecting harmful content on your profile, we securely process certain information through your profile, including:

- Your username and profile info  
- Public posts  
- Public comments  
- Public usernames

So we can automatically flag content to you that may be harmful and crosses your defined threshold.

This data is processed for these limited purposes only. We will not share or disclose this data to any third parties unless legally mandated by a court of law.

By selecting ‘I consent’ you consent to the processing of this information for these purposes. You may change your preferences at any time.
